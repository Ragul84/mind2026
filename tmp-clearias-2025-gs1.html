UPSC Prelims 2025 GS Paper 1 Question Paper Out: Download as PDF

Last updated on May 25, 2025 by ClearIAS Team

UPSC Prelims 2025 GS Paper 1 Question Paper Released. Download UPSC Prelims 2025 GS Paper 1 Question Paper PDF Now.

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducted the Civil Services Preliminary Examination 2025 today, May 25, 2025.

The General Studies Paper 1 (GS Paper 1) was held in the morning session from 9:30 AM to 11:30 AM.

Candidates can now access the question paper PDFs for all sets (A, B, C, and D) to analyse the exam pattern and prepare for future stages.

Exam Highlights

Next Steps
