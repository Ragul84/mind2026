In active voice, the subject does the action: "Ravi wrote the letter." In passive voice, the subject receives the action: "The letter was written by Ravi." For exams, remember that passive often uses a form of 'be' + past participle. Also, in passive, the doer (agent) can be omitted if unknown or unimportant.
